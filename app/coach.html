Peak Plate for your team

Fuel for athletes, not a diet app. Peak Plate helps athletes see what they are actually eating and whether it is enough for the training they do.

What it does

  • Snap a plate, get the calories. Point the camera at a meal and the AI plate scanner names each item, estimates the portion and gives a calorie range with a confidence level. One tap logs it.
  • A target built for training. Pick a goal such as Improve Performance or Build Muscle, enter age, height, weight and activity level, and the app sets a daily calorie target from published sports-nutrition research. The sources are one tap away in the app.
  • Meal ideas that fuel practice. Breakfast, lunch and dinner ideas with a recipe and a grocery list.

How to share it with your team

  1. Post this page, or the link below, in the team chat or the parents' email list.
  2. Ask parents to install it with their athlete at home, so the first scan happens together. Please do not run it as a group session on the school or club Wi-Fi: the scanner limits how many scans one network can make in a day, so a whole team on one connection runs out fast. Mobile data or home Wi-Fi works.
  3. Suggest one habit: scan the post-practice meal for a week, then look at the week's history together.

Privacy, in plain words

  • No account. No sign-up, no email, no name. The app works the moment it is opened.
  • No tracking. No ads, no analytics, no tracking software.
  • Meals stay on the phone. The diary, goals and stats are stored on the device only.
  • Photos are not kept. A plate photo is analyzed and then discarded; it is never stored on our servers, and the AI service that analyzes it deletes it within 30 days (the privacy policy lists the narrow exceptions). Only a small preview stays on the phone with the diary entry.
