Questions

How do I cancel Peak Plate Pro?

iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Peak Plate Pro > Cancel Subscription.

Android: Google Play > profile picture > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions > Peak Plate Pro > Cancel.

Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Why only five free scans?

Each scan runs a paid analysis on our servers, so every install gets five to try it; Peak Plate Pro covers up to 15 a day. Typing a meal in by hand is free and unlimited.

What happens to my plate photos?

A plate photo is analyzed and then discarded. It is never stored on our servers, and the AI service that analyzes it deletes it within 30 days (the privacy policy lists the narrow exceptions). Only a small preview stays on your phone with the diary entry, and it is deleted with the entry.

I paid, but scans are still locked.

Open Profile > Restore purchases. If that does not help, email us with the store (App Store or Google Play) and the date of purchase.
